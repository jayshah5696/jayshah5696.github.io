I liked the concrete case for reading content made for recruiters: it exposes their candid complaints about hiring managers, including advice on what not to do, in a way a polite coffee chat may not. I recommend this as a practical reminder to study the roles you depend on, because that material can reveal the assumptions and failure modes that ordinary cross-functional conversations tend to smooth over.
