I'm not convinced that access to experts by itself gives these vendors a durable edge, but the piece makes a good case for why the opportunity is appearing now. It connects China's big tech "vertical wells" and fragmented enterprise data with labs' growing demand for specialist knowledge and realistic work environments, a sharper explanation than simply saying the country has lots of data.
