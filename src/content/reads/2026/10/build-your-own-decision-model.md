I'd read this for the distinction between choosing quickly and knowing whether the choice is right: masking the vocabulary to A-E cuts the example from 11 decoding passes to one, but the model's 99.78% for Cave on an ambiguous bat question shows how misleading those probabilities can be. The CommonsenseQA results, 59.38% accuracy before a quick fine-tune and 62.41% after, make this a grounded demonstration; I wouldn't treat the apparent confidence as calibrated without stronger evidence.
