I trust this one because curating English improves non-English performance in 12 of 13 languages, while bespoke per-language curation reports gains up to 16.9% over an uncurated baseline. That makes the curse of multilinguality look partly like a data-quality problem, not only a capacity limit; I'd keep the claim bounded because the reported evaluations are multiple-choice benchmarks.
