Always good advice on data labeling. I like the distinction between defining labels and showing how to reach them: Bing’s match‑quality decision tree and Google’s rated examples with explanations give annotators a shared route through ambiguous cases. I’d use the five questions as a drafting checklist, though the examples come from search rating, so they don’t show how much this structure improves agreement on other labeling tasks. I think this is still valid for agentic(LLM) validation.
