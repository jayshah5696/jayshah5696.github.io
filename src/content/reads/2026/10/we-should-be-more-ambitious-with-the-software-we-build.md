The "pre-agentic cost model" is the sharpest idea here: work like speeding up a compiler or building a custom developer tool can now be a reasonable detour, not a years-long bet. I like the pgrust example because its testing has surfaced real Postgres bugs, while the author is careful to distinguish "works for me" from product quality; faster development makes ambitious projects more feasible, not automatically ready to ship.
