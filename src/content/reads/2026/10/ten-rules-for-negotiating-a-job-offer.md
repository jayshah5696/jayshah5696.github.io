I recommend this for its concrete rule that you write down every significant offer detail, including non-monetary facts such as a planned Angular migration and company headcount, then confirm key points by email. I like the companion rule to "always keep the door open": treat each "what do you think?" as a decision point and avoid a premature commitment, so you preserve information and the ability to negotiate. The supplied material covers only some of the ten rules, so the treatment of the back-and-forth and equity is not judgeable here.
