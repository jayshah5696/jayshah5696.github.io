I trust this one because it doesn't treat "open" as a binary: downloadable weights differ from weights plus training data and code for replication, modification, or study. The examples of releases that omit some training data keep the label from doing too much work, though I'd want more than this framing to judge whether any particular model is reproducible or controllable.
