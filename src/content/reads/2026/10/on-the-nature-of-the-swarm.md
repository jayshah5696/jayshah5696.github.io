I'm not sold on the subtitle's inevitability, but the distinction between a context window and "total context capacity" is worth keeping: compaction lets an agent take on larger tasks while making each handoff a bet about what will matter later. The filesystem example sharpens the tradeoff: files preserve details across handoffs, but summaries still need pointers and the live agent has to reread what it needs.
