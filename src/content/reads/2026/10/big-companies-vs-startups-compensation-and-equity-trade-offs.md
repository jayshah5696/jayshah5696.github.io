I liked the piece's refusal to treat startup equity as salary: it compares a conservative big-company path reaching about $1.2M in cumulative compensation within six years with the finding that 80% of YC startup wealth belongs to 0.5% of companies. I recommend opening it for the practical mental model that startup upside is a lottery with liquidity and tax constraints, not simply a premium for accepting lower pay. The example of ISOs expiring 90 days after leaving makes that risk concrete. The supplied excerpt cuts off during the broader comparison, so I can't judge its full treatment of technical work or learning.
