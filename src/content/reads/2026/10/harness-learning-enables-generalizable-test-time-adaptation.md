I trust this one because it asks whether a learned revision procedure transfers, not just whether a harness can be optimized for one task: the proposer uses execution feedback to edit code while both proposer and solver stay frozen at test time. The trained 4B proposer outperforms its 35B teacher on average for single-step revision, and the reported transfer across reasoning and multi-hop QA is encouraging; I'd keep the generalization claim bounded to those settings.
