The author notes that a cache miss during a coding session can be traced manually in 20 seconds through the proxy, providing a useful test of whether observability answers an engineering question rather than merely decorating a dashboard. I like the case for a single endpoint, virtual keys, and per‑request logs, though the post does not weigh that control against the ongoing effort of operating the proxy. Until big labs ban it, I am going to run my own proxy.
