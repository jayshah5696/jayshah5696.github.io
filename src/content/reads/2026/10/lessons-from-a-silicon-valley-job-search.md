I like the advice to "play the ball as it lies" while recognizing that some interview processes are better than others. The funnel of 25 applications, 17 phone interviews, 7 on-sites and 5 offers makes the case for applying broadly, though it's one person's result, shaped by a London-to-San Francisco move and a work visa, not a conversion benchmark.
