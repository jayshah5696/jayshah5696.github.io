I liked that Heaton gives the job search a concrete funnel: 25 applications became 17 phone interviews, 7 on-sites, and 5 offers over six weeks. I recommend opening it for that planning model; it turns job hunting from a vague confidence exercise into a process where you can estimate how much pipeline you need before drawing conclusions from a few rejections. The numbers describe one 2014 search, so they cannot establish current conversion rates.
