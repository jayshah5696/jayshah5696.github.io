I liked the "blast radius" framing because it is grounded in a concrete failure: in 2013, a pricing experiment could not join payments with order data, could not use clickstream JSON, and could not survive a 100m-row query. That makes the stack’s evolution easy to reason about as an expanding reachable boundary, and gives the post-AI claim a practical question: how do data teams encode expert judgment so agents produce analysis against a shared reality? The supplied excerpt ends before the post-AI design is developed, so I cannot judge its implementation details.
