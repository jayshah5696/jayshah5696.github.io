If you're choosing what to work on, I like Fischbach's framing of problem choice as a skill to practice, not just a matter of taste. The "altitude dance" names the shift between focused work and stepping back to update the plan; I especially like the advice to stay clear on the goal while keeping the route flexible, though the article gives a framework rather than evidence that it improves outcomes.
