I liked the "altitude dance": Fischbach says problem solving is 90% doing and 10% switching to a more removed view to update the approach. That makes this worth opening as a practical loop for research or engineering work: pursue a path, then revisit the goal when new information exposes a better one. The supplied material does not show how well the framework works beyond his report that students value the slower process.
