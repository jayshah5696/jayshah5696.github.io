On-policy distillation has the student generate its own trajectories, then uses teacher feedback on those states to address the compounding error that comes from training on flawless teacher prefixes. The survey organizes methods by objective, feedback source, and training dynamics, and compares where they can fail, from diversity collapse to the flawed-prefix trap.
