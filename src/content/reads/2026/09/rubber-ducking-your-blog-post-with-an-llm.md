I recommend this for its concrete interview protocol: the LLM asks one question at a time, gets at most two rounds of sharpening, and checks new answers against earlier ones. That gives you a practical way to use an LLM for thinking rather than first-draft generation, with the conversation ending in an outline and explicit open items.

- The prompt also tells the LLM to flag contradictions once and keep moving, a useful constraint for preventing an exploratory writing session from turning into an endless interrogation. The supplied material does not show results from using it, so its effect on writing quality cannot be judged.
