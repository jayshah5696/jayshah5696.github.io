Most LLM writing advice starts with generation; this prompt has the model interview the author instead, one question at a time, with up to two rounds of follow-up. I like the guardrails that check new answers against earlier ones and make the final outline trace back to what the author said; it's a thoughtful way to use the friction of planning, though the piece offers a workflow rather than evidence that it improves finished posts.
