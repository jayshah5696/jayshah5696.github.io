Paul at its best. I liked the question "what would make this company more powerful?" because the concrete example of agents paying one another turns an agent-payments product into a marketplace rather than merely a better payment tool. I recommend opening it for that mental model: when evaluating a startup, look for a change in position, such as owning the customer relationship, making money flow through the product, or creating network effects, instead of only optimizing revenue from the current product.
