I like this because it treats startup strategy as a search for structural leverage, especially owning the customer relationship or letting others build on the product. The advice to err toward APIs feels newly relevant as agents replace human users, though the essay offers heuristics rather than evidence that network effects or generosity will pay off in a particular market.
