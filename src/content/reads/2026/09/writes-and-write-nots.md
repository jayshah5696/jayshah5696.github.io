I liked the argument that AI's writing escape valve could divide people into "writes" and "write-nots," because it treats writing as a way to think rather than merely a communication skill. I recommend opening it for the concrete warning behind that claim: if writing is where some thinking happens, delegating every draft to AI may leave people less able to reason for themselves. The essay does not establish how widespread that effect will be, so the prediction remains an unresolved question.
