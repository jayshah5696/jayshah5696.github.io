I like the concrete claim that One-Shot On-Policy Distillation lets a large language model keep learning for hundreds of steps from a single query. That makes this worth opening for its challenge to the assumption that on-policy distillation needs many training examples, though the supplied material is too thin to judge the mechanism or results beyond that claim.
