The minimum-editing experiment in “On-Policy Distillation With Different Teachers” is the section to read first. Students distilled from SFT and RL teachers performed similarly, slightly outperformed the RL teacher, and showed only slight forgetting, suggesting that on-policy sampling mattered more than the teacher’s prior performance.
