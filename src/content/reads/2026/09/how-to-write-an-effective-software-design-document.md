I liked the rule that a design doc should focus on decisions by asking, "what’s the penalty for being wrong?" I recommend this as a practical filter: document choices that are expensive or permanent to change, such as choosing C++ over Ruby on Rails, and leave reversible details like pagination for implementation and feedback. It gives reviewers a way to spend attention where it can prevent years of rework.
