I like the test of asking "what's the penalty for being wrong?" The contrast between a costly language choice and a reversible "Load more" button gives a clear reason to focus review on hard-to-change decisions, while the advice to scale the doc to the project's risk keeps the process proportional. The claim that a design doc can save years is asserted rather than demonstrated here.
