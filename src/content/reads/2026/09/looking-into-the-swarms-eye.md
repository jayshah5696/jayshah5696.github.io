I'm not sold on the leap from a token-usage chart to a new era of swarms, but I like the account of where delegation helps, in broad research and data work, with persistent subagents sharing findings across the group. The reported rise from under 100M to over 5B tokens a day makes the scale tangible, while the author admits trajectories are inefficient and Astra can burn thousands of dollars on a simple task; claims about GPT-6's training priorities remain inferences from behavior, not demonstrated facts.
