I liked that this treats LLM-written prose as a reader-retention problem, grounding the point in the cited survey: 78% of developers stop reading when they detect it, and 71% avoid the author afterward. I recommend opening it for that practical mental model: public writing is judged by whether readers trust its authorship, not merely by whether the prose is polished. The Pangram 4 accuracy claims are based on the author's experience, so its detector performance cannot be judged from this entry alone.
