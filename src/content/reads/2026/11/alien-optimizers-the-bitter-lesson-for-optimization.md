I like the argument that optimizer search can escape fixed update rules: Noise's head transplants and Boost → Cut are training‑time interventions, and the challenge reports a 4,000× compute‑efficiency gain in ten days, with a 30M‑token run surpassing backprop. The methods are explicitly hard to interpret, and those results don’t yet show whether the gains transfer to other architectures or tasks. More and more AI writes or discovers things, making it harder for us to interpret.
